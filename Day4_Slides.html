I DO — PYTHAGOREAN THEOREM WITH DESMOS

Let Desmos Do the Math! 🖩

🔍 Hypotenuse Hunter

c = HYPOTENUSE = the LONGEST side = across from the right angle

Always identify c FIRST!

Context: "A TV at AT&T Stadium is 48 in wide and 36 in tall. What's the diagonal?"

🖩 Desmos Steps

Line 1: a² + b² ~ c²   ← tilde, not equals!
Line 2: a = 48
Line 3: b = 36
Line 4: c =   ← leave BLANK!
Desmos shows: c = 60 inches!
Finding a LEG instead? Same method! Just leave THAT variable blank.

If c=13, a=5 → leave b blank → Desmos gives b=12!

I DO / WE DO — TRANSFORMATIONS

Moving Shapes on the Dallas Grid 🗺️

➡️ Translation (Slide)

(x + a, y + b)
"Slide the Cowboys star 3 right and 2 up"
(x+3, y+2)
Point (1,2) → (4,4)

🪞 Reflection (Flip)

y-axis: (−x, y)
x-axis: (x, −y)
"Flip across the Trinity River (y-axis)"
Point (3,5) → (−3,5)

🔄 Rotation 90° CW

(y, −x)
"Turn the Deep Ellum mural 90°"
Point (3,5) → (5,−3)
Quick Memory Trick: Translation = SLIDE (add)  |  Reflection = FLIP (negate one)  |  Rotation = TURN (swap & negate)
