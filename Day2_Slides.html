You earn money for each hour you work PLUS a bonus for setting up!

Building the Equation

What changes? You earn $10 for each hour → that's the slope (m) = 10
What stays the same? You always get $20 for setup → that's the y-intercept (b) = 20
y = 10x + 20
m = MULTIPLIER ($10 per hour)  |  b = BONUS ($20 setup)

I DO — SOLVING EQUATIONS WITH DESMOS

When Are Two Plans EQUAL? 📱

The Scenario

Two phone plans:

Plan A: 15x + 30 (per month)
Plan B: 10x + 55 (per month)

When do they cost the SAME?

🖩 Desmos Steps

15a + 30 ~ 10a + 55
Desmos shows: a = 5

Both plans cost the same at 5 months!

⚠️ NO "x"!

You CANNOT use the letter "x" when solving with tilde!

Desmos treats x as a graphing variable.

Change x to "a" (or any other letter)!

❌ 15x + 30 ~ 10x + 55 → Won't work!

✅ 15a + 30 ~ 10a + 55 → a = 5!

3 Steps:
  1. Change every x to "a"
  2. Type the WHOLE thing with ~ (tilde)
  3. Read the answer!
